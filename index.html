Jacobo (Jacky) Bibliowicz

AI for architecture and design

Research science leader with more than twenty years of work at the intersection of AI, computer graphics, and the built environment — most recently at Autodesk Research, where the work ranged from generative AI systems exploring neuroarchitecture and emotion in spatial design, to real-time data pipelines connecting sensor networks to 3D cities and structures like the MX3D printed bridge. A consistent thread runs through all of it: connecting technology to human experience in physical and built space. The links below are the work itself.

01

Autodesk Research

AI, spatial design & neuroscience
Toronto · 2023 – 2026
01.1

Eudemonial Spaces

2025

An interactive generative-AI art installation exploring spaces through the lens of neuroarchitecture. A camera reads the room in front of it and a generative system composes a new built environment in real time — natural light, views, nature-coloured tones — so that the guests' bodies and the objects around them become the input to the architecture. Presented at Encoding Experience in Boston and San Francisco, featured on the Autodesk Research blog, and accepted as a booth at Autodesk's 2026 internal technology conference.

Bridging design, human sciences, and technology with AI art

01.2

Encoding Experience

2025

A human-centered exploration of the built environment through conversation, emotion, and AI. Speech recordings of single-threaded dinner conversations were broken down into per-speaker remarks, summarized for each participant, and scored for emotion and sentiment. Includes a live sentiment-analysis demonstration delivered at ANFA 2025.

01.3

From Steps to Stories — The Bentway Project

2023 – 2024

An exploration of human experience of public spaces, exploring ways to capture individual experiences of the built environment. Led delivery of instrumented walkshop helmets for data capture at The Bentway park in Toronto and at Autodesk University 2023 — covering networking, Bluetooth sensor communication, and data encryption. Built the video pipeline that anonymized the helmet footage and highlighted facial features for the final art installation, showcased at The Bentway, AU 2023, and Autodesk TechX 2024.

02.1

Dasher360 & Data360

2017 – 2023

Autodesk Research's digital twin platform for buildings. Dasher360 is the front end that loads sensor data from the Data360 database and overlays it on BIM models. Data360 is a Hadoop-based time-series database that powers it. These and their associated projects have reached the end of their life and are no longer on Autodesk's website — the best remaining sources are the links below and on the companion blog.

02.2

MX3D Smart Bridge (collaboration with MX3D)

2018

The world's first 3D-printed steel bridge. Defined the network topology for the local Data360 cluster storing the bridge's instrumentation data, implemented remote cluster management over OpenVPN, and supported the team remotely through the Dutch Design Week unveiling and subsequent load experiments with the University of Twente and Imperial College London.
